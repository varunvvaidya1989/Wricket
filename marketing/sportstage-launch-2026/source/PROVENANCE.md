# Artwork and source provenance

Three original editorial sports images were generated for this pack using the built-in imagegen tool. They are illustrative campaign imagery, not photographs of actual SportStage events. No external stock photographs or human likenesses were supplied. These images do not imply equipment-brand sponsorship.

The SportStage logo is copied unchanged from assets/sportstage/logo/sportstage-mark-rounded-512.png in the application. The October 2026 screenshots (assets/app-home.png, assets/app-start-match.png) are real SportStage web screens captured from the local export at 390×844 @3x without signing in; their sport photos are the app's bundled Pexels images (see mobile/assets/sportstage/sports/SOURCES.md). The earlier setup screenshot (assets/app-match-setup.png, now unused) came from docs/ux-redesign/setup-review.png. IBM Plex Mono is supplied with its license in assets/. Sponsor and score cards in the campaign template are explicitly labelled illustrations. Typography, layouts and diagrams are editable HTML/CSS, exported with a browser.

Space Grotesk and Inter are supplied with their font license files in assets/. The 15-second instrumental bed is newly synthesized by render-videos.cjs, using no third-party samples, recordings or licensed platform tracks. It has no voiceover. The MP4s are motion-graphics films made from campaign layouts, gentle camera movement and crossfades; they are not live-action recordings or app walkthrough recordings.

Carefully distinct accurate objects, elegant natural balanced arrangement in LOWER TWO THIRDS, upper third dark clear negative space for later typography. Camera slightly above, oblique angle. Hard sidelight of mint green #5FE38A and warm cream, dramatic precise shadows, premium sports magazine photography, realistic material details. Faint court line running diagonally. No people, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks. Restrained, authentic, not sci-fi. Keep all equipment within central 80% width. Deliver a single image, not a collage or poster sheet.

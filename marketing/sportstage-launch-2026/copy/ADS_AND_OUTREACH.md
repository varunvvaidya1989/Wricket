## A manageable first paid experiment

Work within the previously discussed total marketing budget of ₹10,000–₹50,000/month. These are optional spending limits for an experiment, not forecasts or a spend authorization.

- At ₹10,000 total: use this pack organically and focus on direct club conversations first.
- At ₹30,000 total: reserve up to ₹10,000 for a single paid channel after the destination and conversion measurement work; retain the rest for partner support and content.
- At ₹50,000 total: reserve up to ₹30,000 for paid media, with the first ₹3,000 as a capped discovery test. Leave the rest unspent until that test has usable activation evidence.

Start with one city you can support, one sport, one audience and two creatives. Choose cricket **or** badminton for that first test. Do not divide a small test budget across every sport and platform. A seven-day discovery test can reveal broken links or weak engagement; it does not establish a reliable acquisition cost or complete platform learning.

Record spend, landing visits, qualified organizer signups, first completed matches and repeat organizers. Define an activated organizer consistently as a new organizer completing a first match within seven days. Compare cost per activated organizer when sample size allows. UTMs identify visits; they do not prove mobile installs or activation by themselves. No revenue or acquisition-cost outcome is assumed.

## Tracking link pattern

Use the verified HTTPS product URL as the base and add:

```
utm_source=instagram
utm_medium=organic_social
utm_campaign=sportstage_india_launch
utm_content=01_brand_feed
```

Change source and content per placement. Paid media uses `paid_social`; club outreach can use `partner`. Do not put personal contact details in tracking tags. These are templates, not live links.

## Sponsor-feature introduction

SportStage lets a tournament organizer add one or multiple sponsors with names, logos and partner labels. This gives the partners behind an event a place in the tournament experience. Would a short preview help you assess it for your next tournament?

This describes the software feature. It is not an offer of SportStage-owned advertising inventory or guaranteed sponsor exposure.

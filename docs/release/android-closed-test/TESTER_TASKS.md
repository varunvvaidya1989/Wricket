# SportStage closed-test handout

Prepared 14 October 2026. This is a task assignment template, not evidence that tasks were performed.

## Join the correct test

The coordinator should copy the opt-in/install link from Play Console -> SportStage -> Closed testing -> Manage SportStage - internal -> Testers and share it with the invited existing cohort. Testers use the same invited Google account in Google Play and remain opted in. The current submitted baseline is 2.0.0, version code 11; a later ad-free candidate needs its own confirmed version. Do not reupload version 11 or use the separate internal-testing track as evidence of the qualifying closed test.

## Confirm participation

Choose 12 willing people from the existing 14-15. Suggested roles: two organisers, four scorers, four captains/players and two spectators; remaining people are backups. Assign a participant ID from the roster template. Confirm device, availability and intended sessions. If fewer than 12 will participate, recruit actual local organisers/scorers/players. No Google rule prescribes this role split or daily-use minutes.

## Meaningful exercises

| Relative window | Exercise | What to report |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1-2 | Install from Play, sign in, choose sports, find/create a competition, join with an invitation | Version/device, access or onboarding problems |
| Days 3-5 | Organiser prepares teams/entries/fixtures; captain manages roster; scorers finish a numeric cricket match; spectator follows it on another device | Confusing steps, score/standings correctness, permission errors |
| Days 6-8 | Assigned racket sports singles/doubles, Undo/server changes; queue scoring offline and reopen/reconnect | Lost/duplicate points or deliveries, readable controls, spectator convergence |
| Days 9-11 | Update through Play after genuine fixes; repeat failed journeys and test account changes | Issue IDs, fixed build, independent retest result |
| Days 12-14 | Complete competition/brackets, inspect stats, repeat sessions, exercise approved recovery/privacy cases | Remaining problems and final experience feedback |

Use controlled test identities for privacy/deletion exercises. Do not delete a real tester's personal account or clear existing app data to test upgrade survival. Email and gesture exercises require the separately pending validation scope; do not record them as passed. Each person follows appropriate role tasks; nobody is instructed to fabricate engagement or feedback. Choose actual session dates after participation is confirmed, not from a guessed eligibility start date.

## Feedback template

Participant ID; app/version; Android/device; task; date; expected outcome; actual outcome; reproduction; screenshot/recording if safe; issue ID; whether a subsequent fix worked. The coordinator reviews issues, records a fix/build and asks a participant to retest. A scheduled task is not a completed task. Keep personal contact information, passwords and tokens out of screenshots and shared reports.

## Find testing information

- Qualifying status: Play Console -> SportStage -> Dashboard -> Apply for production. Record exactly the displayed status/count/dates.
- Invited people and link: Closed testing -> Manage SportStage - internal -> Testers.
- Feedback: Monitor and improve -> Ratings and reviews -> Testing feedback, plus your agreed feedback channel.
- If you meant where to recruit people: begin with your existing cohort and local cricket/racket clubs; recruit people who actually perform these roles and can commit to the exercises.

The coordinator must confirm actual testing and production-access eligibility before reapplying. The tester-list count alone does not establish either.
